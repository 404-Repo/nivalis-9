# NIVALIS 9 // OPERATION 404

An atmospheric first-person stealth game set at an isolated, snowbound relay station. Infiltrate an autonomous defense grid, sever three uplinks, and reach the extraction point.

Watch drone patrols, evade surveillance cameras, and use solid cover as you navigate the yard, service tunnel, and loading deck. Climb access ladders, line up your sights, and choose when to move, hide, or fight. The default Stealth mode rewards patience and careful observation, with additional difficulty profiles offering different ways to approach the operation.

Explore mode lets you investigate the station without incoming fire. Hidden discoveries and achievements reward looking beyond the mission, while mountain winds and an evolving synth soundtrack accompany your journey through the frozen outpost.
