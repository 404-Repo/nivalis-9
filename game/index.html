SENSOR FIELD GUIDE

Drone lamps show illumination, not sensor range. Press M for patrol sectors: H is flight height; R is maximum sensor range.

Solid walls, roofs, and cover block sight. Crouching slows detection. Darkness and visual fog do not provide extra concealment. Graphics settings never change detection.

White underside lights mean patrol; amber means suspicion. A confirmed lock turns them red and the spotting drone moves faster. Red persists during its short last-seen-position search. Break sight to cancel the firing charge; it cannot track you through cover. One drone hit is fatal unless you have the single-use ration buffer.
